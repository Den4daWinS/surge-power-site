What We Do

Elite Staffing Services Across Every Trade

From a single skilled tradesman to a full project crew, our manpower solutions are designed to meet contractors and facility owners where they are: fast deployment, verified credentials, and workers who get the job done.

Journeyman & Apprentice Electrician Staffing

+

Surge Power provides journeyman electricians and apprentice-level support for commercial and industrial projects across PA, MD, and NJ. Every electrician we place is screened for trade experience, safety training, and project-type fit before we ever send them to your site.

Our electrician manpower covers new construction, tenant fit-outs, service upgrades, and ongoing maintenance, giving contractors the flexibility to scale their crews without the lead time and overhead of direct hiring.

General Labor & Site Support

+

From material handling and site cleanup to demo work and equipment staging, our general labor force supports every phase of the job. We provide dependable, safety-oriented workers who take direction well and keep job sites moving efficiently.

General labor staffing through Surge Power is ideal for contractors managing tight project timelines who need cost-effective support without compromising reliability or site safety standards.

Skilled Trades Placement

+

Beyond electricians, our skilled labor pool includes HVAC technicians, pipefitters, sheet metal workers, and other trade-qualified personnel for commercial and industrial projects. We match tradesmen to projects based on experience, certifications, and the specific demands of your job environment.

Our elite staffing approach means we don't just fill a headcount, we find workers whose trade background and skill level genuinely fit the scope of your work, reducing turnover and improving on-site performance.

Equipment Operators

+

We place experienced equipment operators for commercial construction and industrial projects, including forklift operators, aerial lift operators, and light equipment operators with the certifications your site and insurance requirements demand.

All operators provided through Surge Power hold current equipment certifications and OSHA safety training, so you can keep operations moving without the compliance risk of unverified hires.

Foreman & Lead Supervision

+

When you need experienced leadership in the field, Surge Power can staff trade foremen and lead supervisors with a proven track record managing crews in commercial and industrial environments. Our supervisory placements know how to coordinate with GCs, meet daily production targets, and maintain safety culture on active job sites.

Whether you need a working foreman to lead a small crew or a lead supervisor overseeing multiple trades, we can match the right person to your project scope and team dynamic.

Emergency & Surge Crew Deployment

+

Project timelines compress. Crews go short. Deadlines don't move. When you're facing an urgent manpower gap, Surge Power mobilizes quickly, often placing workers within 24 to 48 hours of a request for positions we have active candidates for.
