The Work

What Commercial Electricians Do at Surge Power

Our commercial electricians work on new construction and major renovation projects: office buildings, retail centers, warehouses, schools, medical facilities, and multi-unit residential. Here's what the day-to-day typically looks like:

Rough-In & Framing Phase

Running conduit, pulling wire, installing boxes, and setting panels during the early construction phase before walls close.

Panel & Gear Installation

Setting and terminating distribution panels, switchgear, and service equipment on commercial job sites under Master Electrician supervision.

Lighting & Device Finish

Installing and connecting lighting fixtures, receptacles, switches, and devices per commercial blueprints and specifications.

Low Voltage & Systems

Supporting fire alarm rough-in, data raceway, and low voltage infrastructure alongside the primary electrical scope.

Blueprint & Code Work

Reading and working from commercial electrical drawings, following NEC code requirements, and coordinating with general contractors and other trades.

Inspection Readiness

Preparing work for local electrical inspections and ensuring installations meet the requirements of the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ).

What We Look For

Requirements for Manpower Placement

We hire at every level, from apprentices to licensed masters. You don't need a perfect resume, you need a strong work ethic, reliability, and the right attitude for commercial job sites.

  • Commercial electrical experience preferred (level depends on role)
  • Ability to use common hand/power tools and work on job sites
  • Strong work ethic, reliability, and attention to detail
  • Willingness to follow safety rules and job-site procedures
  • Ability to lift, stand, and work in varying site conditions
  • Ability to follow instructions and work as part of a crew
  • Valid driver's license / reliable transportation
  • Certifications (if applicable): OSHA / Apprenticeship / Journeyman

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about our electrical services, career opportunities, and what it means to work with a team that takes the trade seriously.

What is the commercial electrician salary range at Surge Power Services?
Careers & Pay

Commercial electrician salary at Surge Power Services varies based on licensure level, experience, and the specific markets we serve across Pennsylvania, Maryland, and New Jersey. Journeyman-level commercial electricians typically earn in the range of $28-$42 per hour, while lead and foreman-level roles command higher compensation plus benefits. According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, electricians remain among the most in-demand skilled trades nationally.

We provide competitive, market-rate pay because skilled commercial electrical work demands precision and accountability.

How does industrial electrician salary compare to commercial electrical work?
Careers & Pay

Industrial electrician salary is typically higher than standard commercial rates, reflecting the complexity of industrial systems: motor controls, PLCs, high-voltage equipment, and automated production lines. Experienced industrial electricians in our region can expect $35-$50+ per hour depending on specialization.

Surge Power Services places electricians in both commercial and industrial environments, matching your skill set with the best-paying opportunities available across our client accounts.

Are commercial electrician jobs available through Surge Power Services?
Careers & Pay

Yes. We actively hire for commercial electrician jobs across Pennsylvania, Maryland, and New Jersey, and place qualified tradespeople with our contractor network as well. Whether you're looking for a direct W-2 position on our crew or prefer project-based work, we have pathways for both.

Do you have master electrician jobs or leadership roles open?
Careers & Pay

Master electrician jobs with Surge Power Services are among the most impactful roles we fill. Licensed masters are critical to pulling permits, overseeing inspections, and ensuring our work meets all local municipal licensing and inspection requirements across every jurisdiction we operate in. Pennsylvania has no statewide electrical license: municipal licensing governs each jurisdiction, and you can review applicable requirements through the PA Department of Labor & Industry.

If you hold a master electrician license in Pennsylvania or Maryland, we want to talk. These positions offer leadership responsibility, premium compensation, and direct input into how our field operations run.

What opportunities exist for an electrical journeyman on your team?
Careers & Pay

An electrical journeyman is the backbone of every project we run. At Surge Power Services, journeymen work across commercial and industrial job sites, giving you consistent variety and the chance to sharpen your skills across multiple service lines. All field work follows OSHA construction safety standards, and we support ongoing safety training for all crew members.

We support journeymen pursuing their master's license by offering flexible scheduling around exam prep, and we promote from within whenever project lead and foreman roles open up.

What types of commercial electrical work does Surge Power Services handle?
Our Services

Our commercial electrical work covers the full spectrum: new construction rough-in, panel installations, lighting retrofits, EV charging stations, emergency power systems, and ongoing maintenance contracts.
