What We Do

Industrial Electrical Services Built for Every Phase

Our industrial electrical contractors cover the full project lifecycle, from new facility installation and plant startups to ongoing maintenance and emergency repair across manufacturing and industrial environments.

Industrial Electrician

+

Our industrial electricians are experienced tradesmen who understand the technical and safety demands of plant environments, from high-voltage distribution systems and motor controls to complex panel work and conduit installations in active production facilities.

Whether you need crew support for a plant expansion or a skilled industrial electrician to integrate into your maintenance team, Surge Power provides the right tradesmen with the specific experience your facility requires.

Plant Startup & Commissioning

+

Plant startups demand coordinated electrical execution under tight schedules and high stakes. Our industrial electrical contractors manage the full scope, from rough-in and gear installation to final terminations and commissioning support, ensuring systems come online safely and on time.

We work alongside general contractors, equipment vendors, and facility teams to coordinate power-up sequences, verify installations against drawings, and deliver a facility that's ready to operate from day one.

Motor Control & PLC Wiring

+

Motor control centers, VFDs, and PLC panel wiring require industrial electricians with hands-on experience in automation environments. Our tradesmen read and execute from control drawings with the precision that manufacturing and processing facilities demand.

From new MCC installations to retrofits and control system upgrades, our industrial electricians handle the work accurately and safely, reducing the risk of downtime and ensuring equipment operates as designed.

Industrial Maintenance Electrician

+

Keeping industrial electrical systems running reliably requires more than reactive repairs. Our industrial maintenance electricians deliver scheduled preventive maintenance, system inspections, and rapid-response troubleshooting that keeps your facility operational and minimizes unplanned downtime.

We can embed maintenance electricians directly into your facility's operations team or provide supplemental coverage during shutdowns, turnarounds, and peak production periods, giving you the skilled labor you need without the overhead of direct headcount.

High-Voltage & Switchgear Installation

+

High-voltage electrical work in industrial settings requires experienced contractors with a strict safety culture and the technical competency to execute complex installations correctly. Our industrial electricians handle switchgear, transformers, bus duct, and high-voltage distribution system installations that meet NEC requirements and facility-specific specs.

We coordinate closely with utility companies, equipment suppliers, and project managers to ensure every phase of the installation is executed safely and to specification.

Industrial Electrical Contractors & Manpower

+

Beyond direct contracting, Surge Power provides industrial electrical manpower staffing for contractors and facility owners who need to scale quickly. Whether you're backfilling a crew gap or ramping up for a major capital project, we provide vetted industrial electricians ready to step onto your job site.

Our staffing model gives industrial electrical contractors access to reliable, experienced tradesmen without the overhead of direct hiring, keeping your projects on schedule and your crews at full strength.
