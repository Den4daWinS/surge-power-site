Safety & Capacity Upgrades

Electrical Panel Services & Panel Upgrades

Your electrical panel controls every circuit in your home. When it's undersized, outdated, or failing, the result is nuisance trips at best, and a fire hazard at worst. Surge Power Services provides complete electrical panel services including inspection, repair, and full panel upgrade services for homes throughout Central PA.

If your home still has a 100-amp panel, Federal Pacific or Zinsco equipment, or a panel that can't keep up with modern demand, an electrical panel upgrade brings your system up to current safety standards and gives you room to grow.

Signs You Need a Panel Evaluation

  • Breakers that trip frequently or won't reset
  • Lights that dim when appliances turn on
  • A panel that feels warm or has a burning smell
  • You're adding an EV charger, hot tub, or addition
  • Your home is 25+ years old and hasn't been updated

Common Questions

Residential Electrical FAQs

How much do residential electrical services cost?
Costs vary by the type and scope of work. Small repairs typically run $150-$400. Panel upgrades range from $1,500-$4,000 depending on amperage and complexity. We provide a written, flat-rate quote before any work begins so you know exactly what to expect.
How often should I schedule electrical maintenance?
We recommend a professional electrical maintenance inspection every 3-5 years for homes under 25 years old, and annually for older homes. If you've recently added high-draw appliances or noticed any warning signs, don't wait. Schedule sooner.
Do I need a permit for an electrical panel upgrade?
Yes, panel upgrades require a permit in most jurisdictions in Central PA. Surge Power handles the permit application and coordinates the inspection on your behalf, so the process is seamless for you.
How long does electrical rewiring take?
A full home rewire typically takes 3-7 days depending on the size of the home and accessibility. Partial rewires, a single room or circuit , can often be done in one day. We'll give you a clear timeline during your estimate.
Can you handle home electrical repairs the same day I call?
In most cases, yes. We keep same-day slots available specifically for residential electrical repairs. Call us in the morning and we can often have a licensed electrician at your home that afternoon.
