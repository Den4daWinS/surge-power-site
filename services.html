Who We Are

The Construction Staffing Agency Built for the Trades

Surge Power Services operates as a specialized staffing agency for construction and industrial trades, placing skilled craftsmen on commercial construction projects, industrial facilities, and HVAC/plumbing jobs across the nation. Unlike general construction agencies, we recruit, vet, and deploy tradespeople exclusively: commercial electricians, industrial electricians, HVAC/plumbing technicians, and general labor with hands-on site experience. Demand for skilled tradespeople continues to outpace supply, the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics projects steady growth across construction and extraction occupations through the decade.

Whether you need commercial electrical contractors for a new build, manpower staffing to backfill a crew, or industrial electrical contractors for a plant startup, Surge Power is the single source for every trade discipline your project demands. When the tradesman on your job site needs to show up ready, not learning on the clock, we're the agency that delivers.

Our Process

How It Works

Whether you need a single commercial electrician or a full manpower staffing crew for a large commercial construction project, our process is straightforward, built to get the right tradesmen on your site as fast as possible.

  • Tell Us What You Need+
    Contact Surge Power Services with your project details: trade discipline, crew size, location, timeline, and wage range. The more detail you provide, the faster we can qualify the right tradespeople.
  • We Identify & Qualify Tradesmen+
    Our team sources from our network of vetted commercial electricians, industrial electricians, HVAC technicians, plumbers, and laborers, screening for experience, credentials, and fit with your project requirements.
  • Review & Confirm Placement+
    We present qualified candidates for your review. You confirm the placement, engagement model (contract, temp-to-perm, or direct hire), and start date.
  • Crew Onsite / Project Launch+
    Your tradesman or crew arrives on site ready to work: verified, credentialed, and briefed on your project scope and expectations.
  • Ongoing Support & Closeout+
    Surge Power stays engaged throughout the project. We handle any issues, manage replacements if needed, and ensure a clean closeout when the work is done.
