The Work

What Industrial Electricians Do at Surge Power

Our industrial electricians work in manufacturing plants, distribution centers, food processing facilities, and heavy industrial environments. Here's what the day-to-day typically looks like:

Motor Control & MCC Work

Installing, wiring, and troubleshooting motor control centers, starters, drives, and associated control circuits on industrial production equipment.

PLC & Automation Systems

Supporting PLC panel installation, field device wiring, and I/O terminations for automated production lines and process control systems.

High-Voltage Distribution

Installing and maintaining switchgear, transformers, and high-voltage distribution equipment that powers large-scale industrial operations.

Industrial Conduit & Wiring

Running rigid, IMC, and EMT conduit systems through industrial facilities, pulling wire, and terminating at panels, junction boxes, and equipment.

Maintenance & Troubleshooting

Every industrial electrician technician on our team is expected to diagnose electrical faults, perform planned maintenance, and respond to equipment failures to minimize downtime in production environments.

Inspection & Code Compliance

Preparing industrial electrical work for inspection and ensuring all installations meet NEC requirements and the Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ).

What We Look For

Requirements for Industrial Placement

We hire at every level, from apprentices to licensed masters. You don't need a perfect resume, you need a strong work ethic, reliability, and the right attitude for industrial job sites.

  • Industrial electrical experience preferred (level depends on role)
  • Ability to use common hand/power tools and work on job sites
  • Strong work ethic, reliability, and attention to detail
  • Willingness to follow safety rules and job-site procedures
  • Ability to lift, stand, and work in varying site conditions
  • Ability to follow instructions and work as part of a crew
  • Valid driver's license / reliable transportation
  • Certifications (if applicable): OSHA / Apprenticeship / Journeyman

What is the industrial electrician salary range at Surge Power Services?
Careers & Pay

Industrial electrician salary at Surge Power Services varies based on licensure level, experience, and the specific markets we serve across Pennsylvania, Maryland, and New Jersey. Journeyman-level industrial electricians typically earn in the range of $35-$50 per hour, while lead and foreman-level roles command higher compensation plus benefits.

We provide competitive, market-rate pay because skilled industrial electrical work demands a higher level of technical expertise and accountability than most other trades environments. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics consistently ranks electricians among the highest-compensated workers in the skilled trades.
