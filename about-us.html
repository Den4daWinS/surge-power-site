Our Story

Founded With a Vision for
Commercial Excellence

Surge Power Services was founded by Serge Zaytsev with a clear mission: to build a trades company that commercial and industrial clients could actually count on. Drawing on years of hands-on experience in large-scale electrical and construction projects, Serge recognized a persistent gap in the market, reliable, quality-focused crews who treat every project as if their reputation depends on it, because it does.

From that foundation, Surge Power has grown into a full-service electrical contracting, HVAC/plumbing, and skilled manpower company serving clients nationwide. The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics projects sustained demand for electricians through the coming decade. Surge Power is positioned to meet it. Our team of certified electricians, licensed HVAC technicians, and skilled laborers brings the same standard of precision and professionalism to every job, whether it's a complex industrial installation or a commercial build-out on a tight deadline.

We work directly with general contractors, facility managers, and project owners to understand the specific demands of each engagement, then build the right team around those requirements. No call centers, no revolving subcontractors. Just experienced people who know the trade and take ownership of the outcome.

Our crews bring deep, hands-on experience across commercial electrical, industrial systems, HVAC, and construction labor. We don't put apprentices in charge of your critical project phases, we deploy tradesmen who've been there.

Every installation, every connection, every job site is held to the same standard, one that passes inspection, meets code, and stands up over time.
